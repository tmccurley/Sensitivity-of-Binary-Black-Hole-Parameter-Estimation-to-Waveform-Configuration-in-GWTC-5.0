GWTC-5.0 waveform sensitivity

Purpose

This project examines how inferred binary-black-hole properties can change when the same gravitational-wave event is analyzed with two different waveform configurations: IMRPhenomXPHM-SpinTaylor and IMRPhenomXPNR. Its goal is to make analysis-model sensitivity visible without implying that a single plot settles the underlying scientific question.

The confirmatory comparison covers 18 prespecified GWTC-5.0 events and focuses on chirp mass, mass ratio, effective spin, and luminosity distance, with additional screening and robustness diagnostics.

Author

Thomas McCurley created the analysis and interactive site. The work combines reproducible statistical analysis with a public-facing explanation intended for readers with a range of gravitational-wave backgrounds.
